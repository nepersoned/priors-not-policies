## Problems to fix before submission

1. **AFLRun (2310.12419) is mis-described.** It does not give harder targets more energy. It assigns uniform target weights ("unbiased energy assignment") to counter distance-based fuzzers' bias toward easy targets. Suggested text: "AFLRun instead equalizes energy across targets." Keep LeoFuzz as the "harder gets more" fuzzing example.
2. **Khetan & Oh (2016): "optimal only at high budget" is not in the paper.** It proves the adaptive gain is lambda/lambda_min (collective vs worst-case difficulty) in an asymptotic, large-budget regime. It never shows adaptivity is suboptimal at low budget. The low-budget reversal (spend less on the most ambiguous items) is **Chen, Lin & Zhou**, Opt-KG Sec 8.1/Fig 4. Re-attribute it.
3. **Schaeffer et al.:** "per-problem pass@k decays exponentially" is wrong, because pass@k increases. Write: "the per-problem failure rate (1 - p_i)^k (i.e. -log pass_i@k) falls exponentially in k; the aggregate power law arises from a heavy-tailed distribution of p_i near zero."
4. **CVA (2609.13257):** "no adaptive policy beats uniform" is overstated. Write: "none of twelve adaptive-depth policies beat uniform compute for video world models (Physics-IQ, 3 generators) once the full generation+verification fee is charged". The authors say this is not universal (positive PRM800K case).
5. **Damani et al.:** "tranches" is a single subset (bottom 10% plus top 10% reward variance), Chat domain only, with 25-40% savings vs 0-10%. Do not present them as separate high-variance and low-variance results.
6. **Snell et al.:** the cost-not-accounted claim is correct. Do not imply their policy gives more samples to harder bins: it selects a strategy per bin at a matched budget. Use the ICLR 2025 title if citing ICLR.
7. **Kapoor & Narayanan:** "294 papers / 17 fields" holds only for the Patterns version (the arXiv version says 329), so cite Patterns 4(9):100804.
8. **Venues:** Lightman et al. → ICLR 2024. Brown et al. → arXiv 2024 (no acceptance found). AdaBoN and AFLRun → arXiv only.
9. **Weaver:** cite Saad-Falcon et al., arXiv:2506.18203 / NeurIPS 2025. The NeurIPS (OpenReview) title differs from the arXiv title, so pick one and use it consistently.
10. **Weitzman (1979):** content verified only via a secondary source (primary blocked). **Koopman/Stone:** only the bibliographic data was verified, so check any specific claim against a library copy.
11. **Part A data fixes:** (a) 2607.17454 title is "...via Zero-Shot Geometric *Verification*" (the jsonl says "Evaluation"). (b) 2605.06785 re-uses the IAS rule of 2506.09338 (Park et al. 2025), so it is not an independent instance; flag it or count it once. (c) 2412.20993 (Certaindex) explicitly claims savings on "unsolvable or confidently incorrect" queries, but only for those that become confident. Describe it as having no difficulty-based discount, with low-certainty queries running to the cap. (d) 2608.03961: the hard-prompt floor is in Sec 3.3, not 3.2. (e) 2604.21018: elimination uses a ground-truth oracle.
